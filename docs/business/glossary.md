# Initial Business Glossary — Part 1.1.1

- **Order**: business representation of a customer's request and its progression through ordering. No aggregate/entity classification is asserted yet.
- **Ordering intent**: customer's expressed request to obtain selected items from one restaurant under the current baseline assumption.
- **Acceptance**: restaurant business commitment to fulfill an order, with exact legality deferred to lifecycle modeling.
- **Rejection**: restaurant decision not to accept requested fulfillment.
- **Cancellation**: intent to stop further fulfillment where business rules permit.
- **Payment**: financial aspect of ordering; not synonymous with a service or database record.
- **Refund**: a new financial action that counteracts an earlier financial effect; not erasure of history.
- **Preparation**: restaurant activity performed after the relevant commitment conditions are satisfied.
- **Completion**: terminal business outcome whose exact meaning will be refined in workflow/lifecycle parts.
