# Initial Responsibility Map — Part 1.1.1

| Participant | Initial responsibility |
|---|---|
| Customer | Express ordering intent and required order information; request cancellation when applicable |
| Restaurant | Decide whether requested fulfillment can be accepted; fulfill accepted commitments |
| Payment participant | Establish/reverse financial outcomes when the workflow requires it |
| Platform | Coordinate valid progression and retain the business state needed for later decisions |

These are business responsibilities, not software-component ownership decisions.
