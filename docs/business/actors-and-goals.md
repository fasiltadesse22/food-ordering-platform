# Actors and Goals — Part 1.1.1

## Customer
Goal: obtain selected food from a restaurant under acceptable commercial and fulfillment conditions.

## Restaurant
Goal: decide which requested orders it is willing and able to fulfill, then fulfill accepted commitments.

## Payment participant
Goal: establish financial outcomes required by the ordering workflow. This does not imply a Payment microservice.

## Platform
Goal: coordinate progression toward valid business outcomes while preserving the ordering process's correctness rules.

## Deliberate non-mappings
An actor is not automatically a user account, Java class, bounded context, service, or microservice. Deployment boundaries remain undecided.
